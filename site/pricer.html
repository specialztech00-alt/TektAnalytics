How this is calculated

  • Unit price: the most recent month with a price for that material in the chosen region. If several sources report that month, the median is used and the line says so.
  • Line total: quantity × unit price, rounded to the nearest naira.
  • Escalation estimate (estimate, not a forecast), shown only for materials with at least 3 months of prices:
    1. For each pair of consecutive recorded months, a monthly-equivalent rate (Pnow ÷ Pthen)1 ÷ months apart − 1.
    2. The average of those rates over up to the last 6 recorded months gives g.
    3. Allowance for m months until purchase: (1 + g)m − 1.
  • Prices are as published or quoted by each source. Check the spec, delivery basis and source on each line before using a total. This is evidence for your own judgement, not professional cost advice.
