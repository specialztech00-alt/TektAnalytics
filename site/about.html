About TektAnalytics

A sourced, dated record of Nigerian construction material prices, with tools to use it.

The problem

Construction material prices in Nigeria are scattered across phone calls, WhatsApp groups and one-off reports. The same item can be priced very differently depending on spec, quantity, location and delivery terms. Contractors, quantity surveyors and clients often have no structured, sourced record to estimate, procure or negotiate against.

What this is

TektAnalytics records material prices with their source, spec, region and date, publishes how every number is calculated, and builds simple decision tools on top. It is an early prototype:

  • Live now: the price dashboard, the basket pricer and the methodology.
  • Not built yet: supplier benchmarks, accounts and saved projects, price alerts, forecasts. These are shown as "Coming next" and do not exist yet.

Coverage is small and growing. Every price shown has been checked by hand against its source.

Who is building it

TektAnalytics is the first module of Tektavon, a construction technology venture being built in Lagos by Peculiar Akintoye, a student in the Department of Building, University of Lagos. Tektavon's aim is a set of tools for the construction industry, with TektAnalytics as the shared price data underneath them.
